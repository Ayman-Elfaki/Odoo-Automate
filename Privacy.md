# **Privacy Policy**

## **Overview**
This extension is designed to automate customer selection and invoice creation within your POS system. We take privacy seriously and ensure that no unnecessary data is collected, stored, or transmitted.

---

## **Information We Collect**
**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**  
All automation happens locally in your browser.

The extension:
- Does **not** send data to external servers  
- Does **not** track browsing history  
- Does **not** collect analytics or usage data  

---

## **How Permissions Are Used**

### **tabs**
Used only to detect the active POS tab so automation runs in the correct window.  
No browsing history or unrelated tabs are accessed.

### **webNavigation**
Used to detect when the POS page loads or changes state, allowing the extension to trigger automation at the right time.  
No navigation data outside the POS domain is monitored.

### **host permissions**
Restricted to the POS domain(s) you configure.  
Used solely to read and interact with page elements required for customer selection and invoicing.


## **Data Sharing**
We do **not** share any data with third parties.  
No data leaves your device.


## **Security**
All processing occurs locally within your browser.  
The extension does not use remote servers or cloud storage.


## **Changes to This Policy**
If the privacy policy is updated, changes will be reflected in the extension listing or repository.


## **Contact**
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer.
